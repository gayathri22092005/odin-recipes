Lasagna recipe

Home

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta sheets, rich tomato sauce, cheese, and a flavorful filling. It is baked until the layers come together with a delicious taste and a golden, cheesy top. This comforting meal is popular around the world and is perfect for family dinners and special occasions.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna pasta sheets
  • Tomato sauce
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Italian herbs

Steps

  1. Prepare the sauce by cooking onion and garlic with olive oil, then add tomato sauce and seasonings.
  2. Cook the lasagna sheets according to the package instructions.
  3. Spread a layer of sauce in a baking dish, then add pasta sheets, cheese, and filling.
  4. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used.
  5. Add cheese on the top layer and bake until the lasagna is golden and fully cooked.
  6. Let it cool for a few minutes, then serve and enjoy.
